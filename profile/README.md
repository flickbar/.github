## Flickbar

Snap, resize and move macOS windows with trackpad gestures on their title bars.

Swipe left or right on a title bar to snap a window to half the screen, swipe up to fill it, combine
two swipes for a quarter, rest two fingers for a grid of layouts, pinch to close or go full screen, and hold ⌘
to send a window to another display. A short tutorial teaches the gestures, and Flickbar speaks English, French
and Persian. Free and open source under the GPL.
