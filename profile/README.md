## Flickbar

Snap, resize and move macOS windows with trackpad gestures on their title bars.

Swipe left or right on a title bar to snap a window to half the screen, swipe up to fill it, combine
two swipes for a quarter, pinch to close or go full screen, and hold ⌘ to send a window to another
display. Free and open source under the GPL.

→ **[flickbar/flickbar](https://github.com/flickbar/flickbar)**
